Toh Digital Katora Hai Kya? 🤔

Digital Katora ek playful online experience hai jahan log apne dost, hostel roommates, ya internet community ke saamne apne funny kharche (Chai ka udhaar, WiFi bill, Breakup biryani) ka virtual katora pesh karte hain.

Har donation par koi boring banking OTP screen nahi aati — balki ek 3D sikka hawa mein rotate hota hai, katora khanakta hai, aur receiver ka reaction sad 🥺 se happy 😁 ho jata hai!

Platform ki safety maintain rakhne ke liye har user-submitted katora hamari admin team review karti hai. Hate speech, offensive media, ya illegal content ko turant block kiya jata hai.
